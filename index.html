❓ How to Play

A tiny Minecraft-inspired voxel world.

WASD move · Space jump
Drag mouse / finger to look around (never breaks/places)
⛏️ button — hold to break, tap once to place
1-6 or scroll to pick a block · E or 🧱 Blocks button opens the full block menu
(On touch devices, use the ▲▼◀▶ pad to walk. The ⤴ button in the middle jumps — in Creative, double-tap it to start/stop flying, then hold it to rise and Shift/the down-button area to sink)

🌍 The world is infinite and streams in as you explore — mountains, forests, lakes/sea, waterfalls and wandering animals, in both modes.

🧱 The Blocks menu has ores, stone types, wool, glass, TNT and more, sorted into tabs. New decorative blocks are only mineable/collectible in Survival if you actually break one you placed — most only spawn from the menu in Creative.

📱 On mobile, starting the game will try to go fullscreen and lock to landscape. If it doesn't, open this page in your browser's own tab (not inside another app) and rotate your phone sideways.
